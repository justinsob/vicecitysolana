What is $VICECITY?

A meme coin on Solana, launched on pump.fun on Nov 13, 2026. No utility and no promises. Its pool is paired with $WASTED instead of SOL.

How is it connected to $WASTED?

On-chain. The PumpSwap pool holds $VICECITY and $WASTED, so $VICECITY is priced in $WASTED and trades route through it. You can confirm the quote token on Solscan or DexScreener.

Why pair with $WASTED instead of SOL?

It ties the two together. Liquidity for $VICECITY sits in $WASTED, so activity here shows up there.
